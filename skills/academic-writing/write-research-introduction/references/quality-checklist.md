# Finalization checklist

Score each item `0 = absent/unsafe`, `1 = partial`, or `2 = clear and defensible`. Do not call a draft final if any integrity item scores 0.

## Scientific and citation integrity

- Every statistic, factual claim, and description of prior work has a traceable source.
- Every cited source supports the exact adjacent claim.
- Specific data and findings cite primary research where available.
- Broad background relies on suitable reviews or authoritative sources.
- The draft does not invent citations, consensus, novelty, results, or limitations.
- The gap is bounded and evidenced; absolute “no studies” claims are avoided unless justified.
- The objective, method, result, and contribution match the user's actual study.
- Causal language is used only when the design and evidence permit it.

## Rhetorical structure

- The opening establishes specific relevance rather than empty importance.
- Information narrows from field to exact problem.
- Each paragraph has one dominant role and a useful topic sentence.
- Prior work is synthesized by idea, approach, or conflict rather than listed.
- The gap follows logically from the literature review.
- The consequence or significance of the gap is clear.
- The objective directly answers the gap.
- Optional hypotheses, methods, findings, contributions, and roadmap fit the venue.
- The final one or two paragraphs clearly identify the present study.

## Cohesion and language

- Key technical terms form a consistent lexical chain.
- Demonstrative references have clear antecedents.
- Connectors express genuine contrast, cause, addition, or sequence.
- Paragraph transitions carry forward a relevant idea.
- Present, past, and present-perfect tenses serve distinct functions.
- Claim strength matches evidence.
- Sentences are accurate, brief, and clear without losing scientific conditions.
- The draft avoids copied sentences, inflated novelty language, and unnecessary jargon.

## Venue and presentation

- Length, paragraph count, citation style, person/voice, and content match the target venue.
- Definitions, abbreviations, symbols, and units are consistent.
- Citations are sufficiently distributed for the claims; density is not forced mechanically.
- The Introduction does not duplicate the Abstract or Discussion unnecessarily.
- The requested language, spelling convention, and output format are followed.

## Decision rule

- **Ready:** all integrity items score 2; no structural item scores 0; remaining language edits are cosmetic.
- **Ready with flags:** prose is usable, but named sources or study details still require user verification.
- **Not ready:** the gap, objective, evidence, or study claims cannot yet be defended. Return a move map and targeted evidence requests instead of polished speculation.

## Recommended audit output

Use a compact table:

| Issue | Location | Why it matters | Recommended fix | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then provide the revised text only after high-risk issues have been resolved or explicitly tagged.
