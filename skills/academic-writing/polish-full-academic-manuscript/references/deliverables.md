# Deliverables and working formats

## Choose an editing mode

### Clean edit

Return polished text ready to paste into the manuscript. Preserve headings, citations, equations, figure/table callouts, and formatting markers.

### Annotated edit

Return the clean edit plus a concise change table:

| Location | Original issue | Revision or recommendation | Level | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section/paragraph | Exact issue | Changed wording or action | Correction / Improvement / Author decision | Meaning, logic, cohesion, style, or mechanics |

Include only representative or consequential changes unless the user requests exhaustive tracking.

### Diagnostic report

Use this order:

1. Overall assessment.
2. Argument and claim-evidence issues.
3. Section and paragraph structure issues.
4. Language and cohesion patterns.
5. Terminology and consistency issues.
6. Prioritized revision plan.

## Full-manuscript working ledger

Maintain these compact records while editing:

### Manuscript map

| Section | Function | Main claim/content | Evidence | Link to next section | Issue/action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Terminology sheet

| Concept | Preferred term | Avoided variant | Abbreviation/notation | First-definition location |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Claim-evidence matrix

| Claim | Location | Supporting evidence | Strength supported | Gap or required qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Author queries

Number unresolved questions as `AQ1`, `AQ2`, and so on. State why the answer matters and give a recommended default when safe.

## Long-document protocol

When the manuscript cannot be edited reliably in one response:

1. Read or extract the whole document before finalizing the global diagnosis when possible.
2. Create the manuscript map and terminology sheet.
3. Prioritize structural decisions before sentence polishing.
4. Revise by section or coherent batch.
5. Carry forward decisions and unresolved queries between batches.
6. Reassemble and run a cross-section consistency audit.
7. Distinguish fully checked sections from pending sections.

## Final handoff

Provide:

- the polished manuscript or requested section;
- a short summary of the highest-impact changes;
- any structural recommendations not applied;
- numbered author queries;
- a statement of scope, including what was and was not checked;
- the next recommended pass, such as journal-format compliance or native-domain review.

Avoid generic praise. Tie every assessment to observable features of the manuscript.
