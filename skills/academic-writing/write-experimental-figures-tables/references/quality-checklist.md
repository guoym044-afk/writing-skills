# Delivery checklist

## Evidence fidelity

- [ ] Every number, unit, label, and direction matches the source.
- [ ] Derived differences, percentages, and ratios use the stated reference group and correct formula.
- [ ] No significance, equivalence, causality, mechanism, or generalization is invented.
- [ ] Error bars, confidence intervals, `n`, and replicate types are correctly identified.
- [ ] Each claim points to the panel or table that supports it.

## Selection and visual integrity

- [ ] The chosen visual matches the reader's task: exact values versus trend, relationship, distribution, or process.
- [ ] Axes, units, categories, panels, keys, and statistical annotations are legible and defined.
- [ ] The visual can stand alone.
- [ ] Table orientation and number alignment support comparison.
- [ ] Journal format and final-size readability have been considered.

## Caption and table text

- [ ] The title identifies the experiment or finding rather than saying only "results" or "comparison."
- [ ] Every panel is described in order.
- [ ] Essential system, condition, measurement, encoding, sample, and statistical information is included.
- [ ] Reused or adapted material is cited and permission/license requirements are flagged.
- [ ] The legend describes rather than overinterprets.

## Results prose

- [ ] Each paragraph has one central finding and follows a stable comparison order.
- [ ] The callout is integrated with evidence, not used as empty navigation.
- [ ] Only representative values, trends, and comparisons are narrated.
- [ ] Description, calculation, statistical inference, and interpretation remain distinguishable.
- [ ] Wording strength matches the study design and manuscript section.
- [ ] The paragraph answers an aim or hypothesis and transitions logically.

## Final language

- [ ] Terms, abbreviations, significant digits, tense, and naming are consistent.
- [ ] Percent and percentage points are not confused.
- [ ] `increased by` and `increased to` are not confused.
- [ ] Fold/ratio wording is unambiguous.
- [ ] Missing information is marked with explicit placeholders and listed compactly.
