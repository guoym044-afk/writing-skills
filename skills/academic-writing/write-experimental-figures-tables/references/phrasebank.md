# English phrasebank

Use these as adaptable structures, not fill-in clichés. Replace vague nouns such as `value`, `result`, and `change` with the measured variable.

## Refer to a figure or table

- `As shown in Fig. X, [headline result].`
- `[Headline result] (Fig. X).`
- `Figure XA shows [specific observation], whereas Fig. XB shows [contrast].`
- `The groups differed primarily in [variable] (Table X).`
- `The experimental conditions and corresponding estimates are summarized in Table X.`

Avoid a location-only sentence when it can be joined to the finding.

## Report a value or change

- `[Measure] increased from A to B [units] between [conditions].`
- `[Group B] had a [difference]-unit higher [measure] than [Group A].`
- `Relative to [reference], [measure] was X% higher/lower in [comparison group].`
- `[Proportion] rose by X percentage points, from A% to B%.`
- `[Measure] reached a maximum of A [units] at [time/dose] and then declined.`
- `[Measure] remained stable across [range/conditions].`

## Compare groups, conditions, or trends

- `[Group A] exceeded [Group B] across all tested [conditions].`
- `The two groups were similar at [condition] but diverged after [time/threshold].`
- `The increase was larger in [Group A] than in [Group B] ([direct interaction/comparison statistic]).`
- `Both groups showed [shared trend], although [Group A] displayed greater [magnitude/variability].`
- `Distributions were comparable in [feature] but differed in [feature].`
- `The response increased monotonically with [dose].`
- `The curve plateaued beyond [threshold].`
- `A biphasic response was observed, with [early phase] followed by [late phase].`

## Report statistics

- `[Outcome] was higher in [A] than in [B] (difference, X; 95% CI, L to U; p = P).`
- `The association was positive (estimate = X, 95% CI [L, U], p = P).`
- `The comparison did not reach statistical significance (difference, X; 95% CI, L to U; p = P).`
- `Values are mean +/- SD from n = N [biological replicates/participants].`
- `Shading denotes the 95% confidence interval.`

Replace `+/-` with the target journal's preferred symbol during final formatting.

## State restrained meaning

- `These findings suggest that [bounded inference].`
- `This pattern supports [specific interpretation] under the tested conditions.`
- `The result is consistent with [model or mechanism], although [alternative/uncertainty].`
- `Together, these data indicate [local conclusion].`
- `[Variable] was associated with [outcome]; the analysis does not establish causality.`

## Describe limitations or irregularities

- `Accuracy was lower under [condition], primarily because [documented technical reason].`
- `The estimate was imprecise, as reflected by the wide confidence interval.`
- `This discrepancy was confined to [scope] and did not affect [verified unaffected result].`
- `Because [constraint], the result should be interpreted as [bounded interpretation].`
- `A larger/independent experiment will be required to distinguish between [alternatives].`

## Caption components

- `Figure X. [Objective, comparison, or overall result].`
- `(A) [Sample and measurement]. (B) [Analysis and encoding].`
- `Points represent [unit], bars show [summary], and error bars indicate [SD/SE/CI].`
- `Data are [summary] from n = N [replicate type].`
- `Statistical significance was assessed using [test] with [correction].`
- `*p < 0.05; **p < 0.01; ns, not significant.`

Only include statistical symbols and thresholds actually defined by the analysis.
