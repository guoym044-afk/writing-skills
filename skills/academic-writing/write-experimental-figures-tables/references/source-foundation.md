# Source foundation

This skill distills and operationalizes the two user-supplied teaching PDFs below. It does not reproduce the PDFs.

1. `学习实验图表.pdf`, Chapter 8, “Figures and Tables,” 10 pages. Core contributions: choose visuals by communication purpose; make figures and tables stand alone; call them out in order; use informative titles, legends, labels, panels, statistics, and citations; distinguish figure and table conventions; order visuals to tell the research story.
2. `学习实验图表的引用、数据比较和结果描述.pdf`, Chapter 6, “How to Write the Results,” 14 pages. Core contributions: a four-component Results model; location/technical-description/comment functions; concise, clear, logical Results prose; table-versus-figure decisions; table layout and figure-type guidance; comparison, implication, and limitation language.

The skill adds conservative evidence rules for modern research writing: do not infer statistical significance from appearance, do not invent missing metadata, distinguish percent from percentage points, and match causal wording to the design. Target-journal requirements override generic conventions.
