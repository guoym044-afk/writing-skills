# Final checklist

## Scientific integrity

- [ ] Every factual detail comes from user-supplied material or a clearly cited source.
- [ ] Missing information is marked `[NEEDED]`; no plausible-sounding values were invented.
- [ ] Associations, predictions, and causal effects are labeled according to the study design.
- [ ] Numbers, units, signs, decimal precision, intervals, p-values, and sample sizes match the source.
- [ ] Group, variable, method, and abbreviation names are consistent across Methods, Results, displays, and captions.

## Methods

- [ ] The design, setting, materials/subjects/data, and sequence are identifiable.
- [ ] Critical amounts, conditions, timing, equipment/software, controls, and quality checks are present.
- [ ] Sampling, eligibility, allocation, randomization/blinding, and ethics are addressed when applicable.
- [ ] Established methods are cited; modifications and new methods are sufficiently described.
- [ ] Non-obvious methodological choices have a concrete rationale.
- [ ] Data preprocessing, exclusions, missing data, replicate definitions, statistics, thresholds, and software are reported when relevant.
- [ ] A qualified reader could reproduce the work without guessing at consequential details.

## Results

- [ ] Organization follows the research aims, experiments, or figure/table sequence.
- [ ] Each paragraph has one primary message and identifies its evidence source.
- [ ] Key results include direction, magnitude, units, uncertainty, and statistical/qualitative support when available.
- [ ] Prose highlights representative findings instead of repeating complete tables, figures, or captions.
- [ ] Unexpected findings and null results are reported proportionately rather than hidden.
- [ ] Interpretation remains within the journal's Results convention.
- [ ] Every figure and table is cited in order and every cited panel exists.

## Cross-section coherence

- [ ] Every major result has a corresponding method and analysis.
- [ ] Every consequential method produces a reported outcome or has a clear reason for inclusion.
- [ ] Outcome definitions and analysis populations do not change silently between sections.
- [ ] Methods do not contain results; Results do not introduce undocumented methods.
- [ ] The Results answer the aims or hypotheses stated in the Introduction.

## Language and presentation

- [ ] Tense and voice choices are consistent and purposeful.
- [ ] Sequence markers make procedures easy to follow.
- [ ] Claims use precise verbs and calibrated strength.
- [ ] SI units, symbols, spacing, numerical style, headings, and references follow the target journal.
- [ ] The final draft is concise without sacrificing reproducibility or evidentiary detail.
